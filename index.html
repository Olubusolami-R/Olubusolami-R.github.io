about

I am an MPhil student at the University of Cambridge, on the Speech and Language Processing track of the Machine Learning and Machine Intelligence programme, supported by the Mastercard Foundation Scholarship.

I graduated with a First Class in Computer Science from Covenant University, Nigeria, as a Generation Google Scholar, and I am still early in my research journey. Currently, I volunteer on the Research Team at Machine Learning Lagos.

My research interests are:

  • Trustworthy and calibrated ML
  • Speech and language processing
  • Low-resource and multilingual NLP, with a focus on African languages
  • AI alignment, particularly baking safety into how models are built rather than patching it in after.

Long-term, I want to be where the groundbreaking work happens.
