about

Hi, I'm Busolami (boo · so · lah · me)

I am an MPhil student at the University of Cambridge, on the Speech and Language Processing track of the Machine Learning and Machine Intelligence programme, supported by the Mastercard Foundation Scholarship.

I am drawn to research at the intersection of technology and people, specifically the questions that arise when ML systems meet the real world: who do they work for, how reliably, and under what conditions. My research interests are:

  • Trustworthy and calibrated ML
  • Evaluation and oversight of AI systems in high-stakes deployment
  • Speech and language processing
  • Low-resource and multilingual NLP
  • AI alignment, particularly scalable oversight and evaluation for increasingly capable systems

That same motivation shows up beyond research too. Since undergrad, I have been building and engaging with technical communities across Africa, with a particular interest in creating spaces where people can learn and grow technically. I graduated with a First Class in Computer Science from Covenant University, Nigeria, as a Generation Google Scholar.

Long-term, I want to be where the groundbreaking scientific work happens, and to help build the conditions and rigorous systems for more people to get there too.

2025

ASR Fine-tuning: Wav2Vec2 and WavLM on LibriSpeech

Fine-tuning experiments comparing Wav2Vec2 and WavLM for ASR on the LibriSpeech 10-hour subset. Explored model architecture, layer selection, freezing strategies, and learning rate schedules. Best result: 10.62% test-clean WER using a frozen WavLM backbone with a two-layer feedforward head and the masking hyperparameters from the WavLM paper.

Probabilistic Ranking of Tennis Players

Implemented Metropolis-Hastings MCMC and Expectation Propagation to infer player skills from 1,801 match outcomes. EP achieves comparable accuracy to MCMC 890x faster.
